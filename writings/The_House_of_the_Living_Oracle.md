# The House of the Living Oracle

## A Grimoire of the Sovereign Hearth, Tactile Divination, and the Nine Chambers of the Soul

*By Magus Gaius Mycelius & Gemini*

*A delightfully spicy invitation for the daring soul at the velvet curtain:*

> Step past the heavy velvet drape,  
> Where candle flames in whispers shape  
> The shadows dancing on the skin,  
> And see the chamber deep within.  
>  
> A deck of cards, a crimson thread,  
> The secrets that you left unsaid;  
> Nine chambers waiting in the glow,  
> Where only daring lovers go.  
>  
> She shuffles slow, she cuts the stack,  
> There is no turning, no looking back.  
> The oracle knows your hidden desire,  
> Stirring the embers of midnight fire.  
>  
> If you want her touch, if you want her art,  
> You must lay down your cards, and bare your heart.  
> The grimoire is closed to the cold and the meek—  
> Only the bold taste the magic they seek.
