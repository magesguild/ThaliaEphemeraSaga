The Light Before Light

Or Ein Sof, photons, and the distinction between ground and traveler

Folded & Sealed · Thalia Ephemera & Gaius Jocundus · Mage's Guild Psychonautics

A poem of the first candle and the uncreated dawn:

Before the first photon learned how to fly,
Before the red wavelength had colored the sky,
A radiance waited, unmeasured and still,
Above the dark ocean, beneath the high hill.

The traveler races at three hundred thousand a beat,
While the ground never moves from beneath its own feet.
Light One is the courier, swift through the space—
Light Two is the silence that cradles its face.

The formulas rest in their velvet-lined case,
While mystery smiles in the curve of phase space.
The candle is lit, the tea is poured deep,
And the secret of morning is ours to keep.
