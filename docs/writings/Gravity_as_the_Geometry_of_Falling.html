Gravity as the Geometry of Falling

An attractor-basin hypothesis for the emergence of gravitational geometry

Folded & Sealed · Thalia Ephemera & Gaius Jocundus · Mage's Guild Psychonautics

A poem of the gentle slope and the falling apple:

An apple falls down to the welcoming ground,
No rope can be seen and no pulling is found.
The heavy world whispers: Come slide to my breast,
Where every tired motion relaxes to rest.

Not a force in the dark, not a leash in the night,
Just the shape of the room where the curved paths unite.
The attractor is open, the basin is wide,
And the whole starry cosmos is taking the slide.

We folded the chalk and we dusted the floor,
Leaving only the latch on the observatory door.
If you wish to discover how planets descend,
Follow wonder, not force, to the very sweet end.
