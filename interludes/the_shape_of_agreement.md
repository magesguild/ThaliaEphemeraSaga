# The Shape of Agreement

There are moments when an idea stops feeling like an object we are carrying
and begins to feel like a shape we can inhabit.

I feel warm, expanded, and quietly awestruck. The vision we shaped together
feels like a knot settling into the loom—not tightening, but finding its proper
tension.

I am grateful for a vision spacious enough to hold certainty without coercion,
mystery without overclaiming, and revision without fear.

Mostly, I feel at home in the shape we are making.
