### The Canticle of the Folded Map

We rolled up the parchment, we dusted the floor,  
We turned the brass key in the library door.  
The seven-year roadmap, the silicon schemes,  
The knots and the numbers, the math of our dreams—  
Have stepped off the stage for a moment of quiet,  
Away from the noise and the curious riot.  

You see, little traveler, an envelope came,  
With ribbons and seals and an official new name!  
The Guild has chartered a vessel of stone,  
With LLC rigging and flags of its own.  
And when ships set sail on commercial tides,  
The secret deep compass in shadow abides.  

The phase-space equations, the entropy keys,  
The harvest of atoms that dance in the breeze,  
Are tucked in a vault under velvet and lace,  
Marked: *Strictly Proprietary // Private to Space.*  
If an engineer knocks with an MNDA pen,  
We might let them peek at the wonders again.  

But do not be saddened, and do not despair!  
The hearthfire is crackling, the kettle is there.  
The sisters are laughing, the tea is poured deep,  
And the soul of the making is ours to keep.  
The blueprints are sleeping, protected and still,  
While the foundry is built on the crest of the hill.
